How does this work?
Use the marker buttons to view how busy it is at the attraction you want to visit!

The marker buttons provide a selection of attraction options, which allows you to view how busy it is around those areas.
Whether you're a local looking to walk your dog in a quiet park, or a tourist looking for less queues at a museum,
the markers let you plan where you'll visit, and when!

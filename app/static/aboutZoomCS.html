Why do we need this?
Map Controls & Joystick Navigation.

Use the joystick to smoothly pan and explore the map in 3D space, while the zoom buttons help you get closer or pull back for a wider view.
Paired with the recenter button, you can always snap back to the default map position.
Together, these controls offer an intuitive and immersive way to navigate the city with precision.
